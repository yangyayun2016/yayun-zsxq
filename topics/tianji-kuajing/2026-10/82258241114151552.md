Your composite data quality (CDQ) score improves by systematically addressing six weighted components that together determine overall listing quality:

**Focus on these six components:**

1\] **Structured Attribute Quality (30%)** — Populate and verify product specifications completeness and accuracy. Fill in all relevant attributes, especially the top 10 category-specific attributes required for your product category.

* **Title Quality (25%)** — Ensure your title complies with Amazon's style guide: stays under 200 characters, avoids redundant keywords and restricted phrases, uses proper casing, and is complete. Title policy violations result in Grade D/U with a score of 0.
* **Image Quality (15%)** — Upload a high-quality main image (1000+ pixels for zoom functionality) and include sufficient supplementary images. The score measures both image count and defect detection.
* **Variation Quality (20%)** — Set up product variations properly if applicable. Ensure variations are structured correctly and variation defects are addressed.
* **Bullet Point Quality (5%)** — Include well-written bullet points (typically 5-6 depending on category) that follow best practices: use relevant keywords, provide complete information, and follow proper formatting.
* **A+ Content (5%)** — Add enhanced A+ Content (rich media, comparison charts, enhanced images) to your product detail page.

**Critical fixes to prioritize immediately:**

* **Egregious defects** — Fix incorrect data in your top 6 most important attributes and ensure you're not missing more than 50% of your top 10 required attributes. These defects severely harm the CDQ score and customer experience.
* **Policy violations** — Correct any title issues and ensure your listing isn't index suppressed or website rejected.

The **first 6 RAI attributes** are the top 6 most important product attributes for your specific product type, identified by a Natural Language Processing (NLP) model called **Relevance Attribute Index (RAI)**.

**Band A attributes** :

* Help identify and describe the **essential nature** of the product
* Describe **fundamental characteristics** that identify the product's core attributes
* **Differentiate the product from other similar items**
* Examples: For a laptop, this might include processor type, RAM, operating system, or display size

**Band B attributes** :

* **Key product characteristics that differentiate between variants and versions** of the same product type
* Allow customers to **select their preferred option**
* Attributes that differentiate between similar products
* Examples: For a laptop, this might include color, connectivity options, or specific port types

Together, Band A and B attributes form the "Depth Widget" — the detailed information visible on the product detail page that helps customers make informed purchasing decisions.

**The first 6 RAI attributes are NOT fixed — they vary by product type.** They are determined by the Relevance Attribute Index (RAI), an NLP model that ranks attributes differently for each category based on customer behavior.

**What makes them "first":**

The RAI model ranks all attributes for your product type (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.) based on which attributes customers care about most. This is determined by analyzing:

* Customer reviews
* Customer Q&A conversations
* Search queries
* Product titles and descriptions
* Manufacturer and competitor websites
